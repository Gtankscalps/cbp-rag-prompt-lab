# Visa Waiver Program, ESTA, and the I-94

Citizens of Visa Waiver Program countries can travel to the United States for tourism or business for 90 days or less without first obtaining a visa. Travelers must apply for ESTA authorization before they travel, and they must hold an e-passport with an embedded electronic chip. An approved ESTA is generally valid for two years or until the passport expires, whichever comes first. The Form I-94 is the official record of a traveler's arrivals and departures; most I-94 records are created electronically and can be viewed or printed from the CBP website.

Travelers who have visited certain countries, including Cuba since 2021, may be ineligible for the Visa Waiver Program and may need to apply for a visa instead. Overstaying the 90-day limit can make a traveler permanently ineligible for the program.
