# Bringing dogs and other pets

CDC rules govern bringing dogs into the United States, and the requirements depend on whether the dog has been in a country that is high-risk for rabies in the past 6 months. Dogs coming only from low-risk countries need a CDC Dog Import Form, which is completed online before travel, and the dog must appear healthy on arrival. Dogs from high-risk countries face stricter rules, including rabies vaccination records, a microchip, and arrival through an airport with a CDC-registered animal care facility. Cats are not subject to CDC import requirements, but all animals can be inspected on arrival and denied entry if they show signs of disease.

Other agencies also regulate animals: the USDA restricts some livestock and birds, and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service regulates wildlife and exotic pets.
