# Duty-free personal exemption

Returning U.S. residents can claim an $800 duty-free personal exemption on goods they bring back from abroad, as long as the goods accompany them and are for personal or household use. Members of a family traveling together and living in the same household can combine their exemptions, so a couple can claim $1,600 of goods duty-free. If you exceed the exemption, the next $1,000 worth of goods is assessed at a flat duty rate, and anything beyond that is taxed at the normal tariff rates for each item.

Certain goods do not count against the exemption in the usual way: alcohol and tobacco have their own quantity limits, described in the alcohol and tobacco guidance. Goods that are prohibited cannot be imported at any value, exemption or not.
