# Medications

You may bring prescription medication for your personal use when you travel to the United States. Medication should be kept in its original containers, and you should carry a valid prescription or a letter from your doctor explaining the condition the medication treats. As a general rule, bring no more than a 90-day supply of your medication. The medication must be FDA-approved for use in the United States; some medicines that are legal in other countries are prohibited here. Narcotics and other controlled substances are subject to strict limits, and some require approval from the Drug Enforcement Administration before they can be imported.

Mail-order and internet purchases of prescription drugs from foreign pharmacies are generally not permitted, because the FDA cannot verify their safety.
