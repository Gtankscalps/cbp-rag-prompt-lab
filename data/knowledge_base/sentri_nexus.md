# SENTRI and NEXUS

SENTRI is a CBP program that allows expedited clearance for pre-approved, low-risk travelers crossing the U.S. southern land border from Mexico, using dedicated vehicle lanes at participating ports of entry. Vehicles as well as drivers must be registered and approved. NEXUS is a joint program with Canada that speeds up border processing between the United States and Canada at air, land, and marine ports of entry. The NEXUS application fee is $50, and NEXUS membership also includes Global Entry and TSA PreCheck benefits for U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents.

Both programs require an application, a background check, fingerprinting, and an in-person interview, and both can be revoked if a member violates program or customs rules.
