# Food and agricultural products

You must declare all food and agricultural products you bring into the United States on your CBP declaration form, including snacks in your luggage. Failing to declare agricultural items can result in fines of up to $1,000 for a first offense. Many fresh fruits and vegetables are restricted because they can carry plant pests and diseases. Fresh mangoes are generally prohibited unless they have been irradiated or otherwise treated under USDA supervision. Most meats are restricted, and pork and pork products from countries affected by foot-and-mouth disease are prohibited.

Many everyday items are allowed, including hard cheeses, canned goods, baked goods, candy, and roasted coffee, but everything must still be declared so an officer can inspect it. Declaring an item that turns out to be prohibited does not bring a penalty; the item is simply taken and destroyed.
