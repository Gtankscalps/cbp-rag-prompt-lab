# Prohibited and restricted items

Counterfeit merchandise, such as fake designer bags, watches, and electronics that infringe trademarks, is prohibited and will be seized by CBP; travelers may also face penalties. Absinthe is allowed only if it is thujone-free. Imports of ivory are heavily restricted under wildlife protection laws, and most elephant ivory cannot be imported at all. Drug paraphernalia is prohibited unless it qualifies for a specific exemption. Certain goods from sanctioned countries are prohibited entirely, and items such as firearms, ammunition, and some cultural artifacts require permits from other agencies before importation.

Restricted items are different from prohibited ones: a restricted item may enter with the right permit, license, or declaration, while a prohibited item may not enter at any value.
