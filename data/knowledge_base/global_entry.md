# Global Entry

Global Entry is a CBP Trusted Traveler Program that allows expedited clearance for pre-approved, low-risk travelers upon arrival in the United States. Membership is valid for 5 years. The application fee is $120 and it is non-refundable, even if the application is denied. Applicants must create a Trusted Traveler Programs account, submit an application, and complete an in-person interview at a Global Entry enrollment center after conditional approval. Global Entry membership includes TSA PreCheck benefits at no additional cost.

Travelers with criminal offenses, prior customs violations, or incomplete or false application information may be denied. Members must use the Global Entry kiosks or processing lanes and must declare all goods they are bringing into the country.
