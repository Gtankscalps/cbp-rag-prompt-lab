# Alcohol and tobacco

Travelers who are 21 or older can include 1 liter of alcohol in their duty-free personal exemption; additional amounts may be subject to duty and tax. The duty-free allowance also covers up to 200 cigarettes and 100 cigars. Larger quantities can suggest commercial importation and may require permits and additional screening. Federal rules set the floor, and state laws on alcohol can be stricter, so the rules of the state you enter can further limit what you may bring in.

Alcohol and tobacco products from embargoed countries may be prohibited entirely. Cuban cigars and alcohol acquired in third countries have changed status several times in recent years, so travelers should verify current rules on cbp.gov before traveling.
