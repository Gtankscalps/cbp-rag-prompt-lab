# Currency reporting

There is no limit on the amount of currency you can bring into or take out of the United States. However, if you transport more than $10,000 in currency or monetary instruments on any one occasion, you must report it to CBP by filing FinCEN Form 105. The $10,000 threshold is an aggregate amount: if a family or group traveling together carries more than $10,000 combined, the group must file one report. Reporting is not a tax and the money is not confiscated for being reported; failing to report can lead to seizure and penalties.

Monetary instruments include U.S. or foreign coins and currency, traveler's checks, and bearer instruments such as unsigned checks or money orders made out to bearer.
